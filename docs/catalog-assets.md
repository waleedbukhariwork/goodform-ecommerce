# Catalog imagery and demonstration data

The eight product SVGs in `apps/web/public/products/` were authored for this repository as original vector illustrations. They use no third-party photography or external image service. Versioned `-v1` filenames let a later content change use a new immutable asset URL. Names, prices, descriptions, colors and garment measurements in the seed are fictional demonstration data. Measurements are garment dimensions in centimeters, not a fit recommendation.
